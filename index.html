👤 About Me

I'm a data analyst, AI engineer, and coding teacher based in Tangerang, Indonesia. I graduated from Telkom University in 2025, focused on intelligent systems for my study, and joined Telkom University AI Laboratory from 2023 to 2024. Along my study and career in information technology, I developed a passion in data science and artificial intelligence, while also interested in backend development. From 2025 to mid-2026, I gained an experience as an intern in PT Telekomunikasi Indonesia, in data analysis and web development. Besides of analyzing data, teaching has also enriched my exprience. In 2023, I acted as a practicum assistant in Telkom University Informatics Lab, where I assisted students from 3 different classes for laboratory sessions of Programming Algorithm course and coordinated with other practicum assistants to realize a comfortable learning environment.
